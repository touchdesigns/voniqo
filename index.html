Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Voniqo (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is a standalone media player for Android. This policy explains what information the app does and does not handle. In short: Voniqo does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data.

The short version
  • No accounts with us, no registration, no sign-in.
  • No analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no advertising ID.
  • Everything you enter stays on your own device.
  • We have no servers that receive your data.

Information we collect

None. Voniqo has no backend servers and does not send your information to us or to any third party for analytics, advertising, or profiling. We do not use cookies, advertising identifiers, or any tracking technology.

Information stored on your device

To work as a player, Voniqo stores the details you enter — such as your IPTV provider’s server address, username and password, or an M3U playlist URL, plus app preferences, favourites and playback positions — locally on your device only. This information:

  • is kept on your device so you don’t have to re-enter it;
  • is sent only to the IPTV provider you configure, in order to log in and stream the content you request;
  • is never transmitted to Voniqo or to any other party by the app;
  • is removed when you delete the account in the app or uninstall Voniqo.

Content and third-party providers

Voniqo does not provide, host, or control any channels, playlists, movies, series, or other streaming content. You supply your own legally obtained playlist or streaming source. Your use of a third-party IPTV provider is governed by that provider’s own terms and privacy policy, not this one.

Permissions

Voniqo requests internet access so it can connect to the provider you configure and stream media. It does not request access to your contacts, location, microphone, camera, photos, or call logs.

Children

Voniqo is a general-audience media player and is not directed at children. An optional parental PIN is provided so you can lock chosen categories on your device.

Data security

Because your information never leaves your device through the app, it is protected by your device’s own security. We recommend using a device lock screen. Note that connections to a third-party provider use whatever security that provider offers.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date above shows when it last changed. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
